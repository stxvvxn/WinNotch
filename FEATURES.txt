WINNOTCH - FEATURE LIST
=======================

A MacBook-style notch / Dynamic Island for Windows. It sits at the top of your
screen; hover over it (or tap it) to open it.
Single portable exe, no install needed. Everything can be switched on/off in Settings.


THE NOTCH
---------
- Small black notch at the top of the screen that expands when you hover over it
- Clock, always visible (also when open)
- Weather icon (current conditions for your location, no setup needed)
- Battery icon, with a lightning bolt while charging
- Camera and microphone in-use dots (like the green/orange dots on a Mac)
- Red mic icon while your microphone is muted
- Choose the size (small / normal / large), width (narrow / normal / wide)
  and which screen it appears on (main, follow the mouse, or a specific display)
- Always on top of everything, with an optional auto-hide for full screen
  apps, games and videos
- Start with Windows
- Tray icon: Settings, Hide/show the notch, Check for updates, Close


NOW PLAYING
-----------
- Song, artist and album art from Spotify, browsers, Apple Music etc.
- Play / pause, next, previous
- Progress bar you can click to skip through the song
- Animated sound bars while music plays
- Briefly pops open when the song changes (optional)
- Hides itself when nothing is playing (or turn it off completely)


POP-UPS (DYNAMIC ISLAND STYLE)
------------------------------
- Volume change
- Screen brightness change (laptops)
- Charger plugged in
- Microphone muted / unmuted
- Timer finished


SHELF
-----
- Drag files onto the notch to keep them there, drag them out again to
  copy them somewhere else
- Remembered between restarts
- Right-click: open, show in folder, remove
- Image actions (right-click an image): convert to PNG, convert to JPG,
  resize to a set width, compress, copy image
  (resize width, JPG quality and where new files are saved are all adjustable)


QUICK ACTIONS FOR YOUR APPS
---------------------------
When one of these apps is in front, the open notch shows shortcut buttons for it:
- Photoshop:     Quick Export, Export As, Save As, Transform, Fit Screen, Undo
- CapCut:        Split, Delete, Play/Pause, Undo, Import, Export
- Chrome / Edge: New Tab, Reopen Tab, Back, Reload, Downloads, Private
- Firefox:       New Tab, Reopen Tab, Back, Reload, Downloads, Private
- File Explorer: New Folder, Rename, Copy Path, Up, New Tab, Properties
- Word:          Save As, Bold, Comment, Word Count, Track Changes, Replace
- Outlook (classic and new): New Email, Reply, Reply All, Forward, Mark Read, Calendar
- Discord:       Mute, Deafen, Switcher, Search, Mark Read, Upload
- Claude:        New Chat, Explain, Summarise, Fix Writing, Code, New Task
- Steam:         Big Picture, Library, Store, Downloads, Friends
- Xbox app:      Game Bar, Screenshot, Record That, Record, Mic
- Fully editable (actions.json): add your own apps, shortcuts, programs or links


TOOLS (BUTTONS ALONG THE TOP OF THE OPEN NOTCH)
-----------------------------------------------
- Clean up: clears temp files, crash dumps and browser caches
- Screenshot to shelf: Snipping Tool or ShareX (Ctrl+PrintScreen)
- Colour picker: click anywhere on screen to copy its colour
- Timer, stopwatch and Pomodoro (with a chime when time is up)
- Clipboard history: click anything you copied earlier to copy it again
- Claude buttons: new chat, explain / summarise / fix what you last copied
- Mic mute: mutes every microphone in Windows (Teams, Discord, Zoom, games),
  also with a shortcut that works anywhere (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M, changeable)
- Quick notes: a notepad that saves as you type
- Calculator and converter:
    sums           12*4+3, 15% of 80, 200 + 20%, sqrt(2), 2^10
    units          5 ft in cm, 70 f to c, 3 stone to kg, 2 gb in mb, 10 mph to kph
    currencies     20 usd to gbp, £50 in eur (live daily rates)
    Enter copies the answer


BUBBLES
-------
- Round shortcut buttons that pop out around the open notch
- Defaults: Camera, Terminal, Pictures, Documents, Music
- Add your own (ready-made presets or any folder, app or link) with a choice of icons
- Arrange them by dragging, with separate layouts for the tall, short and mini notch


SYSTEM STATS
------------
- CPU use, memory use and network speed (down / up) in the open notch


THEMES
------
- Accent colour: 9 presets or any colour you like
- Notch style: black, graphite, glass (see-through) or black with an outline
- Optionally colour the progress and volume bars too


HANDHELD MODE (ROG Ally / Ally X, ROG Xbox Ally, Steam Deck, Legion Go, MSI Claw...)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Detects which handheld you have, or pick it yourself
- Tap to open, bigger buttons for fingers
- Controller navigation: hold View + Menu, or a back button mapped to Ctrl+Alt+N
- Quick glance over games
- Battery details: percentage, time left, power draw graph, battery health
- Low battery warnings (shown even over games)
- Brightness and volume sliders
- Quick switches: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Airplane mode, refresh rate, power mode,
  resolution (720p / 1080p), on-screen keyboard, Game Bar, stay awake, screen off
- Download speed, and stay awake until a download finishes
- Automatic profiles: max Hz + Performance when plugged in,
  60 Hz + Efficiency on battery
- Per-game profiles (remembers refresh rate and power mode for each game)
- Play session stats


UPDATES
-------
- Checks GitHub for new versions and offers to update itself
  (shows what's new; Update now / Later / Skip this version)
- Updates install in a few seconds and WinNotch restarts by itself
