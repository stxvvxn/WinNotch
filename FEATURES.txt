WINNOTCH 2 - FEATURE LIST
=========================

An Apple-style notch for Windows. It sits at the top of your screen; hover over
it to open it. Single portable exe, no install needed. Everything can be
switched on or off in Settings.


THE NOTCH
---------
- Closed: the date (DD/MM/YYYY) on the left, the time (24-hour) on the right
- Hover to open: the full date (e.g. Friday 9 October 2026), a search box and
  your home page underneath
- Moving the mouse away, pressing Esc, or the notch hiding always takes it back
  to the home page
- Round page bubbles under the open notch switch between pages: home, music,
  volume and shelf (the one you're on lights up in your accent colour)
- Choose the size, width, style (black, graphite, glass, outline), accent
  colour and which screen it appears on
- Optional hide for full screen apps, games and videos
- Start with Windows; tray icon with Settings, Hide notch, Check for updates


SEARCH BOX
----------
- Apps and files on the PC (through the Windows Search index), with icons
- Search the web (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Ecosia or your own link)
- Ask an AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, Gemini or your own link)
- Enter opens, Shift+Enter searches the web, Ctrl+Enter asks the AI,
  Esc clears and then closes; Ctrl+Alt+Space opens it from anywhere
- Maths and conversions: type a sum (or "15% of 80", "5 miles in km") and the
  answer appears at the top - Enter copies it
- Reminders and timers (see below)


HOME PAGE
---------
- Quote of the day
- Weather for your town (Open-Meteo, free): temperature, conditions, high and
  low, chance of rain - click for the full forecast
- Your next calendar event from an Outlook, Google or iCloud calendar link,
  including repeating meetings - "Now · until 15:30", "Today 15:00 · in 2 hr"
- Day progress bar with the week number: your working day or the whole day
- Quick toggles: mute mic, do not disturb, dark/light mode, Bluetooth,
  keep the PC awake, lock the PC (they light up when on)
- Countdown to your next reminder or timer, with a cancel button
- System info: CPU, memory, GPU, disk, network, battery, up time - as tiles or
  one compact line. Click the disk tile to flick between drives; the disk bar
  turns orange at 70% full and red at 80%


REMINDERS AND TIMERS
--------------------
- Type them in the search box: "remind me in 20 min to check the oven",
  "remind me at 5pm call mum", "remind me tomorrow 9am bins"
- Repeating: "every weekday at 9am stand-up", "every day 8pm meds",
  "every 45 min stretch"
- Timers: "timer 10 min", "5 minute timer for eggs"
- Or add them in Settings → Reminders (once, daily, weekdays, weekly, every
  few minutes/hours)
- When one is due the notch drops open with a bell and a sound, and stays
  until you press Done or Snooze (5 min / 1 hr; +1 / +5 min for timers)
- The closed notch counts down to the next one ("in 12 min", or 09:59 for a
  timer) - and to your next calendar event


MUSIC PAGE
----------
- Song, artist, album and artwork from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music,
  Amazon Music, TIDAL, Deezer, browsers and more
- Play/pause, previous, next, shuffle, repeat and a seek bar
- Choose your music service; Spotify gets Like and Smart Shuffle buttons


VOLUME PAGE
-----------
- Whole-PC volume, plus a slider and mute button for each app making sound


SHELF PAGE
----------
- Drop files on the notch to keep them, drag them out again (move or copy,
  like File Explorer)
- Picture previews, right-click menu that matches the notch
- Screenshots (Snipping Tool, Win+Shift+S, PrintScreen, ShareX) land on the
  shelf automatically and the notch pops open to show them
- Scratch pad for quick notes, saved as you type


BUBBLES
-------
- Round shortcut buttons around the open notch (sides and bottom) for your
  apps, folders and websites - arrange them in Settings → Bubbles


UPDATES
-------
- Checks GitHub for new versions and offers to update itself (Settings →
  General → Updates, or right-click the tray icon → Check for updates)
